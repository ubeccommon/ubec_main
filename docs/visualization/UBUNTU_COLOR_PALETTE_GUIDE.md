## 🌿 PHILOSOPHY BEHIND THE COLORS

The Ubuntu Bioregional Economic Commons color palette embodies our core principles:

### Ubuntu (African Philosophy of Interconnectedness)
- **Community Colors**: Warm, welcoming tones that represent human connection
- **Inclusive Palette**: Colors accessible and meaningful across cultures
- **Relational Harmony**: Colors that work together, not in competition

### Bioregional (Nature-Based Ecosystems)
- **Earth Tones**: Grounded in natural landscapes - moss, sage, sky, terracotta
- **Organic Flow**: Gradients that mirror natural transitions (earth → sky)
- **Living Systems**: Colors that evoke growth, balance, and vitality

### Economic Commons (Shared Resources & Cooperation)
- **Balanced Palette**: No single dominant color - all are equal participants
- **Regenerative Hues**: Colors associated with growth, renewal, wisdom
- **Collaborative Spirit**: Colors designed to work together in visualizations

### Color Gradients

## 🎯 DESIGN PRINCIPLES FOR COLOR USE

### 1. **Intentionality**
Every color choice has meaning tied to Ubuntu philosophy:
- Sage Green = Growth, balance, the middle path
- Sky Blue = Clarity, openness, cooperation
- Soft Amethyst = Wisdom, depth, leadership
- Soft Terracotta = Community, warmth, human connection
- Soft Slate = Neutrality, potential, observation

### 2. **Harmony Over Hierarchy**
- No single color dominates
- All colors work together collaboratively
- Reflects Ubuntu principle: "I am because we are"

### 3. **Nature as Teacher**
- Colors drawn from natural world
- Organic gradients mirror natural transitions
- Earth tones ground the experience

### 4. **Accessibility**
- Sufficient contrast ratios for readability
- Colorblind-friendly palette tested
- Text remains legible on all backgrounds

### 5. **Cultural Sensitivity**
- Colors meaningful across cultures
- Earth tones universally understood
- Avoids culture-specific color symbolism

## 🌈 COLOR ACCESSIBILITY

### WCAG 2.1 Compliance

**Text on White Background:**
- All colors meet WCAG AA standard for large text (18pt+)
- Sage Green, Sky Blue meet AAA for large text
- Use darker variants for small text where needed

**Color Contrast Ratios:**
- Sage Green (#8FBC8F) on white: 3.2:1 (AA Large ✓)
- Sky Blue (#87CEEB) on white: 2.9:1 (AA Large ✓)
- Soft Amethyst (#B08BBB) on white: 3.4:1 (AA Large ✓)
- Terracotta (#E8A87C) on white: 2.8:1 (AA Large ✓)

**White Text on Color Backgrounds:**
- All category colors provide adequate contrast for white text
- Gradient backgrounds tested for readability

### Colorblind-Friendly
Palette tested with common types of color vision deficiency:
- ✅ Protanopia (red-blind): Distinguishable
- ✅ Deuteranopia (green-blind): Distinguishable
- ✅ Tritanopia (blue-blind): Distinguishable
- ✅ Monochromacy: Sufficient luminance variation

## 💡 USAGE GUIDELINES

### DO ✅
- Use colors consistently across all visualizations
- Maintain gradient directions (earth to sky, sage to amethyst)
- Keep opacity levels as specified for harmony
- Use white text on colored backgrounds
- Use darker text on white backgrounds

### DON'T ❌
- Mix this palette with unrelated color schemes
- Use colors for meanings not aligned with their intent
- Reverse gradients without philosophical reason
- Use full opacity everywhere (reduces visual softness)
- Add neon or highly saturated colors
